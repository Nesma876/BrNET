\section*{Table S2: matched-area perturbation sensitivity analysis}
The recovered table contains 2,594 correctly classified images with masks. A valid matched control was unavailable for 86, leaving 2,508 pairs. Parentheses give patient-clustered 95\% confidence intervals from 2,000 bootstrap replications (seed 123).
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Perturbation & Class-change rate & Probability reduction \\
\midrule
Tumor region & 0.0625 (0.0438--0.0841) & 0.0380 (0.0261--0.0524)\\
Matched control & 0.0044 (0.0017--0.0077) & 0.0014 (0.0005--0.0022)\\
Paired difference & 0.0578 (0.0398--0.0790) & 0.0371 (0.0249--0.0520)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Exact control-region coordinates and complete hashes/provenance of the 15 generating checkpoints were not retained. This is a partially traceable sensitivity analysis, not evidence of causal explanation faithfulness.
